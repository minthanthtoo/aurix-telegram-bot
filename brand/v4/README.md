# AuriX Telegram entity avatars V4

Three related but distinguishable avatars for Telegram:

- `aurix-telegram-bot-avatar-v4.svg` — segmented digital signal ring for `@aurix_outline_vpn_bot`.
- `aurix-telegram-group-avatar-v4.svg` — three connected nodes for the admin/community support group.
- `aurix-telegram-channel-avatar-v4.svg` — outward signal waves for `@AurixDigitalStore`.

## Crop and recognition rules

- Full-bleed midnight background: no white or transparent outer margin.
- Essential mark remains inside an approximately 82% diameter safe zone.
- Outer differentiation survives Telegram's circular crop.
- No words, initials, or tiny interface symbols are used; they become illegible in chat lists.
- The official AuriX AX geometry and gradients are unchanged.

Use the 1024 px PNG exports for upload. The 512 px exports are included for preview and lightweight integrations.
