## Audience study

This post addresses people who normally rotate through free VPNs but will remember a dependable paid option when access matters. The giveaway is not positioned as proof of permanent uptime. Its job is to let five people experience a full paid-size allowance with almost no entry friction, while everyone else discovers the Bot, support group, and announcement channel.

Primary situations:

- heavy social users who have outgrown small free allowances;
- students, freelancers, creators, developers, and online sellers who need a larger VPN allowance;
- cautious buyers who want first-hand experience before paying;
- current free users who could later convert when continuity matters.

## Commercial brief

- Audience tension: `အခမဲ့ VPN တစ်ခုပြီးတစ်ခု စမ်းနေရပေမယ့် ပိုသုံးကြည့်ဖို့ မယုံသေးဘူး။`
- Objective: earn qualified reach and first-hand product trial without engagement bait.
- Primary promise: five people receive one 100 GB AuriX Outline-compatible key each.
- Product memory: `100 GB · ၅ ယောက် · ရက် 30 · AuriX`.
- Reason to believe: exact allowance, duration, transparent random selection, named Telegram support paths.
- Entry: comment `100GB စမ်းမယ်` once on the Facebook Page post.
- Primary metric: valid comments, Telegram channel visits, and later paid conversion from participants.

## Territory exploration

1. Prize cards in a pricing grid — rejected: resembles a website section and repeats the category's template look.
2. Gift box with confetti — rejected: generic giveaway language with weak VPN/product memory.
3. Five floating neon keys — rejected: generic AI-tech aesthetic and literal security-key confusion.
4. Human phone screenshot — rejected: fake-interface risk and too much setup explanation.
5. **AuriX access envelope with five signal passes — selected:** a tactile branded envelope releases five distinct access passes; the front pass carries the official Outline Client icon. It makes the quantity and product physical without pretending the image is interactive.

## Plan council — first judgment

The initial plan required Page follow, post sharing, friend tagging, and Telegram joins. The council rejected it because it added friction, looked like engagement bait, and conflicted with Meta promotion rules governing personal timelines and friend connections. It also made the three Telegram destinations feel like chores rather than useful pathways.

## Improved and locked plan

- One comment is the only entry action.
- No purchase, share, friend tag, or follow is required.
- The artwork contains only the prize, product/duration clarification, winner count, and one comment instruction.
- Full rules, closing time, selection method, SIM-data clarification, fraud warning, platform release, and affiliation note remain in the caption.
- Telegram roles are distinct: Bot for trying/buying, Group for human help, Channel for winner announcement and service news.
