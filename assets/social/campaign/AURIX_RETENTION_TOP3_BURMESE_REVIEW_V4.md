# AuriX Retention Top 3 — Burmese and Visual Review V4

Batch: `20260828-162238`

## Overall judgment of V3

V3 visual hierarchy was usable, but the Burmese often read like English product terminology inserted into Myanmar sentences. `Error screenshot`, `key`, `plan availability`, `payment review`, and `quota enforcement` raised reading effort and made the voice less local and less reassuring. The old visual plates also communicated generic help or a gateway rather than the exact retention event.

## Post 1 — Setup recovery

- V3 weakness: `Error screenshot နဲ့အတူ Telegram မှာ မေးပါ` is functional but sounds translated and does not tell the customer what information support needs.
- V4 lock: `ချိတ်ဆက်မရဘူးလား။ ဖြစ်နေတဲ့နေရာကို ပုံနဲ့ပြောပါ။`
- Improvement: gives three concrete inputs, adds a privacy warning, and changes the image from an abstract support scene to a tangled signal becoming clear through human help.

## Post 2 — Data-limit recovery

- V3 weakness: `key ကို ရပ်မယ်` is system-centered and can sound punitive. `Quota enforcement သည် server metrics...` is unsuitable customer-facing Burmese.
- V4 lock: `ဒေတာပြည့်သွားရင် ဘာဆက်လုပ်မလဲ။`
- Improvement: explains that the current connection stops, reassures the user that the phone/app is not broken, and shows an empty measured reservoir leading to new plan choices.

## Post 3 — Renewal

- V3 weakness: `သက်တမ်းတိုး` may imply that the current entitlement extends automatically. `Plan availability` and `payment review` are English-heavy.
- V4 lock: `ဆက်သုံးမယ်ဆို ကြိုစီစဉ်ထားပါ။`
- Improvement: accurately asks the user to check the end date, choose the next plan, and wait for payment confirmation. The new image shows an approaching end marker and a continuing path without promising uninterrupted access.

## Language gate

The V4 copy is a materially more natural working version. A Myanmar native copy reviewer should still approve dialect, tone, and service-specific terminology before paid distribution.
