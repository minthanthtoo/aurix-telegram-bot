# AuriX retention language review V9

## Judgment of the previous hooks

`သုံးခွင့်မကုန်ပါစေနဲ့` and `သက်တမ်းမကုန်ပါစေနဲ့` were understandable but sounded like translated warning copy. They instructed the viewer negatively and did not mirror how a customer naturally describes the problem.

## Approved question-led hooks

1. `Outline VPN ချိတ်မရလို့ စိတ်ညစ်နေလား။`
2. `AuriX VPN ဒေတာ မလောက်မှာ စိုးရိမ်နေလား။`
3. `VPN သက်တမ်းကုန်တော့မှာ စိုးရိမ်နေလား။`

These hooks begin with the user's present worry, retain explicit VPN product memory, and invite the caption to provide the solution.

- connection failure: a real user, phone, laptop, tangled signal and visible community-help transition;
