# AuriX Outline content review V8

## Council judgment

### Product clarity

Approved flow: `AuriX Key → official Outline Client → Connect`.

This resolves the earlier ambiguity about what the VPN product is. The customer should remember that AuriX supplies the access key while Outline Client is the app used to connect.

### Language

- Removed standalone `ဒေတာပြည့်` language.
- `လက်ကျန်` is always qualified as the allowance for the AuriX key used in Outline Client.
- `သက်တမ်း` refers to the AuriX access key, not the Outline app.
- `Outline app သွင်းထားရုံနဲ့ မရသေးပါ` explains that an app without a key and server does not create a working VPN connection.

### Trust and affiliation

Every caption states that AuriX is independent and is not an official Outline Foundation partner. The official Outline icon is used as product identification, not as an endorsement badge.

### Safety

- Captions point to official Outline downloads only.
- Users are told to copy the complete key beginning with `ss://`.
- Complete keys must not be posted publicly or included in screenshots.
- No real or sample working access key appears in any artwork.

### Visual system

The guide posts are editorial infographics, not app screenshots. Numbered steps are appropriate because the content is explicitly instructional. No step is styled as a fake clickable button.

Native Burmese review is still recommended before paid-media deployment.
