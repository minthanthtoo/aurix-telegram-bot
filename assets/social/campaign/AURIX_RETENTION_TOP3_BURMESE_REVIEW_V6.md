# AuriX retention top 3 — final V6 review

## Content architecture

- Artwork stops the scroll: one problem, one emotional resolution, one concise benefit.
- Caption converts interest: exact plan facts, real Telegram destinations, payment expectation, and safety guidance.
- Telegram performs the action: bot for trial/purchase/status, group for support, public channel for announcements.

No artwork contains a fake button, numbered setup steps, privacy instructions, terms panels, or placeholder links.
