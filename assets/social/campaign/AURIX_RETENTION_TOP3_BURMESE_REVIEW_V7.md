# AuriX retention top 3 — Burmese review V7

## Strategy lock

- Audience: existing or trial AuriX VPN users who may lose access because setup fails, the VPN allowance runs out, or access expires.
- Core promise: prepare and get help before AuriX VPN access is lost at the moment it is needed.
- Product memory: every artwork names VPN or AuriX VPN; no standalone “ဒေတာပြည့်” wording remains.
- CTA role: the image creates relevance; the caption routes to Usage, Status, purchase, or human help.

## Language correction

Rejected: `ဒေတာပြည့်သွားပြီလား။`

Reason: Burmese readers can reasonably interpret it as a SIM/mobile-data package problem. It does not identify AuriX VPN or the specific allowance being discussed.

Approved replacement: `VPN လိုတဲ့အချိန် သုံးခွင့်မကုန်ပါစေနဲ့။`

The supporting line identifies the measurement as `AuriX VPN ဒေတာလက်ကျန်`. The caption explicitly distinguishes this from phone SIM data and mobile-internet packages.

## Exact image copy

1. `VPN ချိတ်ဆက်မရလို့ ရပ်မနေပါနဲ့။`
2. `VPN လိုတဲ့အချိန် သုံးခွင့်မကုန်ပါစေနဲ့။`
3. `VPN သုံးနေရင်း သက်တမ်းမကုန်ပါစေနဲ့။`

Native Burmese approval is still recommended before paid media deployment.
