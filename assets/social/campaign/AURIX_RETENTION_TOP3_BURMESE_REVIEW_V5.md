# AuriX retention top 3 — Burmese and creative review V5

Status: artwork approved for final QA; captions await the real Telegram bot link.

## Council decision

V4 communicated too much inside the artwork. Its cards, numbered steps, pill-shaped calls to action, and safety notes made each post resemble a website screenshot. V5 separates the jobs:

- Image: stop the scroll with one relevant visual, one headline, and one benefit.
- Caption: explain the next step, safety guidance, and service details.
- Telegram bot: perform the real action after the user taps the post link.

No V5 artwork contains a fake button, a numbered procedure, plan terms, or an instruction such as “do not include…”.

## Burmese language judgment

### 1. Setup help

Final headline: `အခက်အခဲရှိရင် AuriX ရှိတယ်။`

Why it works: short, reassuring, conversational, and human. The support line explains the practical value without turning the image into a troubleshooting guide.

### 2. Data finished

Final headline: `ဒေတာပြည့်သွားပြီလား။ ဆက်သုံးဖို့ လမ်းရှိတယ်။`

Why it works: begins with the user's immediate problem and resolves anxiety in the next sentence. The wording is familiar spoken Burmese and avoids technical vocabulary.

### 3. Renewal

Final headline: `ဆက်သုံးဖို့ ကြိုတင်စီစဉ်ထားပါ။`

Why it works: direct but not alarming. It does not promise automatic or uninterrupted renewal; the support line gives the concrete behavior to take.
