# AuriX Online Seller Access — Creative Plan

## Objective

Convert Myanmar Facebook Page sellers and social-commerce operators who lose time testing VPNs before replying to customers. Lead with the work interruption; use quota lookup and wallet coverage as purchase reassurance rather than the opening claim.

## Reference study

The five supplied KBZPay ads were treated as visual evidence only.

Transferred principles:

- a large, rounded Burmese display hierarchy readable in one glance;
- a light aqua/pastel field with one warm accent;
- one concrete usage scene instead of an abstract technology background;
- controlled symmetry in the hook and intentional asymmetry in the hero scene;
- generous negative space around the promise;
- one quiet proof/footer region.

Deliberately rejected:

- KBZPay royal-blue ownership, partner-logo hierarchy, promo ribbons and prize/confetti language;
- crowded offer stacks and coupon-like controls;
- generic glowing servers, shields, dashboards or fake app UI.

## Explored territories

1. **Seller packing desk — selected.** Parcels and message phone establish the commercial occasion; tangled routes visualize repeated VPN testing; one cyan route ends at the AuriX Bot.
2. **Reply queue editorial poster.** Strong typography but too close to app-interface advertising.
3. **Parcel-label collage.** Distinctive, but quota and payment proof became visually fragmented.

## Reading path

1. `Customer စာပြန်ချိန်`
2. `VPN လိုက်စမ်းရတာ`
3. `မောနေပြီလား?`
4. AuriX Outline VPN Key + Bot capability
5. Large Bot identity and five-wallet proof

## Brand translation

- AuriX navy carries authority and body copy.
- Cyan is the working route and system signal.
- Amber highlights only the friction phrase.
- Outline is a recognition anchor, not presented as a co-equal partner.
- Payment marks are normalized inside equal rounded containers; no provider is made dominant.

## Typography and mobile constraints

- Canvas: 1080 × 1350, 4:5.
- Primary Myanmar family: Noto Sans Myanmar UI with real installed weights.
- Myanmar text includes language and mark-positioning metadata.
- Hook baselines and region clearances exceed the campaign's conservative Myanmar-script floors.
- Final thumbnail is inspected at 324 × 405.

## Publication caveat

The ad does not promise universal connectivity or speed. The caption states that results can vary by network, ISP and current server conditions, and avoids implying an official Outline or wallet-provider partnership.
