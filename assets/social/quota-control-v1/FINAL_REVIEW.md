# AuriX Quota Control — Final Review

## Review loop

### R0 — mechanism and message

- Kept the central promise narrow: know the key's usage before access stops.
- Replaced dashboard and fake-UI concepts with one memorable quota instrument.
- Established the reading order: pain point, product pairing, visibility, warning levels, Bot destination.
- Issue found: the feature block was dense and the Bot identity looked secondary.

### R1 — hierarchy and identity

- Enlarged the Bot destination and replaced the naive robot badge with the professional AuriX Bot seal.
- Strengthened the three warning collars as the visual mnemonic for quota alerts.
- Reduced decorative elements so the image reads as an advertisement rather than a product screen.
- Issue found: the middle feature rail still competed with the warning message.

### R2 — final polish

- Compressed usage, remaining quota, and expiry into one line.
- Made `My VPN` the single action destination without drawing a fake button.
- Clarified the caption as rolling 30-day transfer accounting, not a live speed meter or calendar-month ledger.
- Retained only verified warning thresholds: 25%, 10%, and 5%, followed by a separate quota-reached notice.
