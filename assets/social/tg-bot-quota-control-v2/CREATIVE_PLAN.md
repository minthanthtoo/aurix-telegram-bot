# Telegram Bot Quota Control V2 — Creative Plan

## Job

Retain paid professional and technically aware customers by making Outline VPN quota visible before it reaches zero.

## Audience and message

- Audience: online sellers, freelancers, operators, and experienced VPN users who value predictability more than free-only access.
- Pain: Outline itself does not present the AuriX account's remaining quota or AuriX warning thresholds in the desired customer workflow.
- Promise: an AuriX-bought VPN Key can be checked in My VPN at any time; Telegram warns at 25%, 10%, and 5% remaining and separately when quota is reached.
- Hook: `Outline VPN Key ရဲ့ / Quota ဘယ်လောက်ကျန်သေးလဲ?`
- Proof: `25% · 10% · 5% / ကျန်တိုင်း Telegram ကနေ ကြိုသတိပေး / AuriX Bot မှာ ဝယ်ထားတဲ့ VPN Key / My VPN မှာ အချိန်မရွေး ဝင်စစ်`

## Visual hierarchy

1. One technical question at the top.
2. One large A-shaped quota instrument with three meaningful checkpoints.
3. One warning sentence in a clear band beneath the instrument.
4. One large approved Bot avatar with an attached Telegram badge.
5. One lookup proof line and the Bot handle.

This is a dark precision lane within AURIX-SOCIAL-S1. It must feel operational and authored, not like a generic AI technology background or a software dashboard.

## Guardrails

- Do not call the quota `mobile data` or use ambiguous `data ပြည့်` wording.
- Do not show a fake live speed meter or imply monthly-calendar reset behavior.
- Keep the three real warning thresholds exact: 25%, 10%, and 5% remaining; quota reached is a separate notification.
- Payments and plan prices belong in the caption, not the feature-focused image.

## R3 plan judgment

R2 was factually correct but opened like a technical feature label. R3 reframes the hook as the user's real uncertainty, enlarges the approved Bot avatar, and gives the warning sentence its own mark-safe region. A fake dashboard, notification screenshot, payment strip, and generated cyber scene were rejected because each would weaken the single quota-control memory.
