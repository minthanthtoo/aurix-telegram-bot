# AuriX Bot Launch — 100GBFREE V1 Final Review

## Review loop

### R0

The visual mechanism succeeded immediately: the A-shaped portal made the brand ownable and exactly five tangible passes made the limited offer self-evident. The dominant weakness was typographic compression. The launch sentence reached too far toward the portal and the footer action shared one crowded baseline.

### R1

The keyword gained stronger dominance and the footer became a separate action zone without becoming a fake button. The five passes remained countable at full size. The composition passed the overall flow review.

### R2

Upper/lower Burmese clearance was increased, the launch line and product qualifier were separated, the Bot avatar was enlarged, and the action became a four-step reading sequence: `ရယူဖို့` → `Bot ထဲမှာ` → `100GBFREE လို့ ပို့လိုက်ပါ` → handle. At 324 × 405, the hero keyword, five-pass mechanic, Outline cue, and Bot destination remain legible in that order.
