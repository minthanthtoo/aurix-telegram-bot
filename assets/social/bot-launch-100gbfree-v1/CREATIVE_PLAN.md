# AuriX Bot Launch — 100GBFREE V1

## Locked campaign role

- Audience: Myanmar users who need a dependable VPN path and have not already opened or completed an AuriX paid order.
- Job: turn the public Bot launch into an immediate, memorable Bot start.
- Promise: the first five eligible Telegram accounts that send the exact keyword `100GBFREE` receive one 100 GiB Outline VPN key valid for 30 days.
- Product memory: AuriX Telegram Bot provides Outline VPN keys; `100GBFREE` is a real Bot trigger, not decorative copy.
- Primary action: open `@aurix_outline_vpn_bot` and send `100GBFREE` exactly.
- Primary metric: valid Bot starts carrying the exact keyword.

## Truth gates

1. The campaign is first-come-first-served, not a random draw.
2. The key quota is VPN usage, not SIM/mobile data.
3. Existing open/completed paid-order accounts are ineligible.
4. A winner's account is terminal under the current product rules: later free, paid, renewal, and replacement entitlements are disabled. This must appear in the caption before publication.
5. A provisioning failure does not consume a winner slot.
6. Publication requires a live test of the exact `100GBFREE` path against production infrastructure.

## Reading path

1. Event: `AuriX Telegram Bot စတင်အသုံးပြုနိုင်ပါပြီ`
2. Value: `100GBFREE`
3. Product: `Outline VPN Key · သက်တမ်း ၃၀ ရက်`
4. Scarcity: `ပထမဆုံး ၅ ဦးအတွက် အခမဲ့`
5. Action: `Bot ထဲမှာ 100GBFREE လို့ ပို့လိုက်ပါ`
6. Destination: `@aurix_outline_vpn_bot`

## Visual territories explored

- Giveaway confetti and gift box — rejected; generic and attracts low-intent freebie traffic without product memory.
- Phone/dashboard showing a Bot chat — rejected; becomes a website screenshot or fake interface.
- Cyber shield and server racks — rejected; generic VPN advertising and hacker-coded distrust.
- Five floating pricing cards — rejected; reads as a pricing UI rather than a launch event.
- Monumental A-shaped launch portal releasing exactly five tactile access passes — selected; the brand geometry, five-slot mechanic, and launch motion become one ownable visual mechanism.

## Plan review and improvement

Initial plan placed `100GBFREE` before the product and made it the only dominant message. Review found that this would over-index on freebie traffic and leave `Outline VPN Key` unclear. The locked version leads with the Bot launch, then uses the keyword as the memory device, immediately qualifies it as a 30-day Outline VPN key, and makes the five-account limit explicit. The image stays minimal; material eligibility and terminal-account conditions remain in the caption.
