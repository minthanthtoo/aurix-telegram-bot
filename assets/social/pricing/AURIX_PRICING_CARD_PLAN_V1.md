# AuriX Pricing Card Plan V1

Date: 2026-08-29  
Format: Facebook / Telegram portrait static, 1080 × 1350  
Product: AuriX independently supplied Outline-compatible VPN access keys

## Audience and job

- Primary: Myanmar mobile users who need VPN access but hesitate over cost, setup, and whether the allowance will be enough.
- Secondary: Existing free users who are ready to move to a paid plan without comparing technical specifications.
- Conversion job: Make the four choices understandable in under three seconds, then move purchase intent to `@aurix_outline_vpn_bot`.
- Retention job: Set accurate expectations around allowance and 30-day validity so customers choose the right plan and avoid early disappointment.

## Locked product ladder

| Role | Allowance | Validity / renewal | Price | Customer cue |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| Quick try | 300 MB | 24 hours; claim daily | Free | အရင်စမ်းချင်သူ |
| Longer try | 3 GB | 30 days; claim every rolling 30 days | Free | ကြာကြာစမ်းချင်သူ |
| Everyday paid | 50 GB | 30 days | 3,000 ကျပ် | ပုံမှန်သုံးသူ |
| More-use paid | 100 GB | 30 days | 6,000 ကျပ် | ပိုသုံးသူ |

Use `ရက် 30 တိုင်း` instead of `လစဉ်` for the free 3 GB entitlement. This avoids implying a calendar-month reset. Do not describe AuriX VPN allowance as mobile-data balance.

## Initial plan

1. Present free plans as low-risk entry, not as bait.
2. Make `50 GB · 3,000 ကျပ်` the visual recommendation because it is the easiest paid step.
3. Keep `100 GB · 6,000 ကျပ်` equally honest but secondary; do not manufacture a discount because both plans have the same unit price.
4. Put allowance, validity, and price together on every paid card.
5. Use one non-button CTA line: `ဝယ်ယူရန် @aurix_outline_vpn_bot`.
6. State `Outline Client ဖြင့် အသုံးပြုရန်` once so the product category is clear without turning the pricing post into a setup guide.

## Council judgment — pass 1

### Conversion judge

The initial plan is accurate, but four equal cards would create choice overload. The paid decision needs to be visible before the viewer reads every free entitlement.

### Myanmar-language judge

`ပုံမှန်သုံးသူ` and `ပိုသုံးသူ` sound more natural than formal package labels. `ရက် 30 အသုံးပြုနိုင်` is clearer in-image than a long expiry explanation. Detailed renewal terms belong in the caption.

### Trust judge

Avoid `အကောင်းဆုံး`, `အမြန်ဆုံး`, `unlimited`, and artificial savings badges. There is no factual basis for those claims. Use `ရွေးချယ်မှုများ` rather than a fake interactive button.

### Mobile-feed judge

The viewer needs a large price anchor, a large quota, and only one short qualifier per card. Telegram handle can be a footer, not a button-shaped control.

## Improved and locked plan

- Visual hierarchy: headline → two paid cards → compact free-trial strip → Outline compatibility line → Telegram destination.
- Product memory: `50 GB / 3,000 ကျပ်` and `100 GB / 6,000 ကျပ်`, each for 30 days.
- Style A — **Clear Choice**: crisp comparison cards, calm premium utility, 50 GB marked `အများဆုံးရွေးလေ့ရှိ` as a merchandising cue only if the business accepts it. For this release, use the safer label `စတင်ရွေးချယ်ရန်` rather than an unverified popularity claim.
- Style B — **Data Journey**: tactile measured-data vessels and stronger brand-world imagery; cards remain readable and are not rendered as fake app UI.
- Exact headline: `သုံးမယ့်ပမာဏနဲ့ ကိုက်တာကို ရွေးပါ။`
- Paid card labels: `ပုံမှန်သုံးသူ` / `ပိုသုံးသူ`.
- Free strip: `အရင်စမ်းချင်ရင် — နေ့စဉ် 300 MB · ရက် 30 တိုင်း 3 GB အခမဲ့`.
- Product clarification: `AuriX key ကို Official Outline Client ဖြင့် အသုံးပြုရန်`.
- CTA role: destination line only, `ဝယ်ယူရန် — @aurix_outline_vpn_bot`; no fake button.

## Generation and review loop

For each style:

1. Generate a text-free visual plate.
2. Composite exact Burmese and numeric content deterministically.
3. Judge first-frame clarity, brand memory, product relevance, copy space, claim safety, cultural fit, and render quality.
4. Improve one named weakness and regenerate.
5. Repeat the judgment and improvement once more.
6. Keep the second regeneration as the final publish candidate; retain iteration notes and sources.

## Publication gate

Before publishing, confirm live availability of both paid plans, receipt-review staffing, quota enforcement, and successful Outline key provisioning. Native Burmese review remains advisable before paid media spend.
