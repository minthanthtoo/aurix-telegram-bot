## Scoring method

Each candidate was scored from 1–5 for first-frame clarity, brand memory, product relevance, copy space, claim safety, cultural fit, and render quality. Brand memory, claim safety, or cultural fit below 4 is a rejection.

## Final council decision

Style A should launch first for direct conversion. Style B should follow 3–5 days later for visual variety or be used for warm retargeting. Do not publish both on the same day; they carry the same offer and would compete rather than extend the campaign.

Claims remain deliberately narrow: allowance, 30-day validity, price, free entitlement cadence, and Outline Client use. No speed, security, anonymity, unlimited, discount, or popularity claim was added.
