# Setup Rescue V1 — Creative Plan

## Job

Prevent first-use abandonment after the customer receives an Outline Key by providing a clear, human support path.

## Audience and message

- Audience: Myanmar users who have received their Outline Key but are struggling to connect (e.g. wrong app, OS issues).
- Friction: Don't know what to do next, technical confusion.
- User-provided product promise: Send a screenshot of the error to the AuriX Group, and a human Admin will help you.
- Hook: `Outline Key / ထည့်မရဘူးလား? / ဒီအဆင့်လေးပဲ`
- Proof: `Screenshot တစ်ခု ပို့ / AuriX Group မှာ မေးပါ`

## Visual hierarchy

1. Hook question at top left.
2. Hero scene: A physical map on a desk bridging a laptop and phone, showing a clear cyan path to a golden help point.
3. Proof card outlining the 2 simple steps.
4. Bot avatar and Telegram Group link at the bottom.

## Guardrails

- No fake chat UI or generic AI neon city.
- Reassuring, human, editorial aesthetic.
- Must use official logo assets (V2 AuriX mark, Outline official green icon, V4 Bot avatar).
- No wallet marks (this is a support post, not a payment post).

## Production method

The text-free editorial plate was generated using AI image generation to achieve the premium photographic standard. Then, the approved logos, exact Burmese copy, and typographic hierarchy were composed deterministically in SVG and rendered via a Python script, exactly matching the `receipt-confidence-v1` pipeline.
