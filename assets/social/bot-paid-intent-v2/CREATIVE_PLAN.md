# AuriX Bot Launch V2 — Generative Creative Plan

## Audience and job

Audience: Myanmar professionals, freelancers, students, developers, creators, and online sellers who already know why they need a VPN and value time more than endlessly rotating through free apps.

Job: introduce the AuriX Bot as the quick, understandable path from payment receipt to an Outline-compatible key.

## Locked message

1. Tension: `အလုပ်လုပ်ဖို့ VPN ရှာနေရင်း အချိန်ကုန်နေလား?`
2. Solution: `AuriX Bot ကနေ စလိုက်ပါ`.
3. Reason to act: `ပုံမှန်ဆို ပြေစာစစ်ချိန် ၁ မိနစ်အောက်*`.
4. Destination: `@aurix_outline_vpn_bot`.

Plans, five payment methods, daily free allowance, support links, and operational caveats remain in the caption.

## Explored visual territories

- Bot avatar plus vector flow — rejected as the main direction because the earlier version read like an assembled infographic.
- Fake Telegram conversation — rejected because it resembles UI and implies interactive controls.
- Generic server/connection imagery — rejected because it is category-generic and may imply unsupported performance.
- Single professional portrait — generation attempted, but the built-in image generator reached its temporary usage limit.
- **Generated documentary before/after plate — selected fallback:** the existing generated plate contrasts searching on a phone with returning to laptop work. It directly visualizes the time-cost tension and uses a human context instead of cards or diagrams.

## Production rule

The generated image owns the people, setting, light, and emotional contrast. Exact copy, official AuriX/Outline marks, the speed qualifier, and destination are overlaid deterministically. No plans, wallet names, explanatory labels, or fake buttons appear in the artwork.

## Plan review and improvement

The initial plan carried two prices, five wallet marks, free allowance, the Bot flow, speed, and Outline recognition in one image. Review rejected it because those facts competed equally. The revised post has one job—Bot introduction—and moves offer inventory into the caption.
