# AuriX Quota Control + Payments — Creative Plan

## Locked brief

- Audience: tech-savvy Myanmar professionals and existing Outline VPN users who dislike invisible quota use and interrupted work.
- Job: make AuriX memorable as the control layer around an Outline VPN Key, then remove payment-method friction.
- Tension: Outline Client does not warn the customer about AuriX quota thresholds or expose AuriX entitlement context by itself.
- Promise: check used and remaining quota in My VPN at any time; receive Telegram warnings at the implemented 25%, 10%, and 5% remaining thresholds.
- Product memory: large AuriX Bot identity connected to a clearly recognizable Outline Client mark.
- Conversion proof: KBZPay, WavePay, AYA Pay, uabpay, and CB Pay are accepted transfer methods.
- Destination: `@aurix_outline_vpn_bot`.

## Reading path

No more guessing → Outline Key controlled through AuriX Bot → check anytime → threshold warnings → familiar payment methods → Bot destination.

## Explored territories

1. Generated quota instrument — rejected after user review because the glossy object looked synthetic and did not explain the product.
2. Dashboard or usage cards — rejected because it would resemble a website or fake interface.
3. Authored editorial-vector system — selected. Product marks become the focal mechanism; two open feature regions and a normalized wallet rail carry exact information.
4. Typographic poster without product marks — rejected because it weakened Outline and Telegram Bot recognition.

## Plan review and improvement

The initial vector plan used the same sans-serif voice everywhere and a small text label to explain the Bot. The revised plan gives one short Burmese hook to `Noto Serif Myanmar`, keeps factual lines in `Noto Sans Myanmar`, enlarges the visual Bot glyph, enlarges the official Outline icon, and removes the explanatory Bot label. Payment icons use equal outer cells, explicit rounded inner clips, and optical padding; their native artwork is preserved.

## Claim boundaries

- Current implementation uses warning thresholds at 25%, 10%, and 5%, plus a separate quota-reached notice.
- Do not advertise 50%, 10%, and 0% until implementation and tests change.
- Usage is based on the latest available rolling 30-day Outline transfer metrics, not live speed or a calendar-month ledger.
- Wallet marks identify accepted transfer methods; they do not imply partnership or automated integration.
