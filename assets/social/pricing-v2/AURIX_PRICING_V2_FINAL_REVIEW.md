# AuriX Pricing V2 — Final Review

Campaign stamp: `20260829-165212`  
Status: replaces the V1 glossy pricing-card direction

## Outline compatibility refinement — R3

The official Outline Client icon is now visible in both final directions so a first-time viewer can recognize which app receives the AuriX key.

- Street Print: treated as a slightly rotated physical app sticker. It belongs to the pasted-poster material system and does not resemble a button.
- Real Life: placed as a clean top-right editorial app mark, balancing the AuriX service logo at top-left.
- Hierarchy: AuriX remains the primary seller/service identity; Outline Client is the secondary compatibility cue.
- Caption guard: explicitly states that AuriX is not an official Outline Foundation partner and independently supplies Outline-compatible access keys.

## Direction 1 — Street Print

| Round | Judgment | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| R0 | Strong non-web composition and excellent price memory; texture still felt mostly passive. | Improve material rhythm. |
| R1 | Added print energy, but black roller ink interfered with the headline and 50 GB offer. | Reject and constrain ink to the edge. |
| R2 | Clean information fields, tactile paper, irregular physical layers, no UI grammar, strongest feed interruption. | Final. |

Final scores: clarity 5, brand memory 4, product relevance 5, claim safety 5, cultural fit 5, non-generic visual character 5.

Use first for launch/reach. The oversized quota and price create memory before the viewer reads supporting terms.

## Direction 2 — Real Life, Two Rhythms

| Round | Judgment | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| R0 | Human relevance was strong, but the glowing center line reintroduced generic AI-tech styling. | Remove the glow and flatten the photography. |
| R1 | More observational and credible; a residual colored seam still read as digital styling. | Replace with a physical print gutter. |
| R2 | Two lived-in usage rhythms, physical editorial split, readable open typography, no panels or fake controls. | Final. |

Final scores: clarity 5, brand memory 4, product relevance 5, claim safety 5, cultural fit 5, non-generic visual character 5.

Use as the follow-up for consideration/conversion. It helps customers identify with a usage level rather than compare abstract packages.

## What is deliberately absent

- pricing cards, rounded panels, pill labels, buttons, browser frames, dashboards, or app screenshots;
- glass capsules, glossy 3D technology objects, neon server environments, shields, locks, or hacker imagery;
- generated Burmese lettering;
- popularity, discount, speed, security, anonymity, or unlimited claims.

## Recommended sequence

1. Street Print on Facebook for first exposure.
2. Real Life, Two Rhythms 3–5 days later on Facebook and Telegram.
3. Compare Telegram bot starts per 1,000 reached users and paid orders, not likes alone.
