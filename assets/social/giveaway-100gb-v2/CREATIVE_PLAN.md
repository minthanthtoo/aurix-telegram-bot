# AuriX 100 GB Giveaway V2 — Generative Creative Plan

## Audience and job

Audience: Myanmar users who rely on free VPNs but may become paying users after experiencing a full-size allowance.

Job: make the giveaway understood in three seconds and create one low-friction action.

## Locked message

1. Prize: `100 GB`.
2. Eligibility/quantity: `၅ ယောက်အတွက် အခမဲ့`.
3. Product: `Outline VPN Key · ရက် 30`.
4. Action: `“100GB စမ်းမယ်” လို့ Comment ရေးပါ`.

All rules, Telegram destinations, SIM-data clarification, fraud warning, and disclaimers belong in the caption.

## Explored visual territories

- Documentary user portrait — rejected for this post because it does not make five prizes visually memorable.
- Generic gift box/confetti — rejected because it could advertise any giveaway.
- Vector access envelope — rejected as final production because earlier versions became mechanically assembled.
- **Generated tactile editorial still-life — selected:** five blank physical passes emerging from a midnight paper envelope. The exact count is visible before reading; paper grain and imperfect edges prevent a synthetic UI-card look.

## Generation strategy

The image model owns materials, shadows, pass spacing, envelope construction, and atmosphere. It receives no text, logos, numbers, or interface instructions. Exact copy and official marks are composed afterward.

The first generated plate passed inspection: exactly five passes, no fake text, useful negative space, believable paper texture, and no generic AI/VPN clichés. It was accepted without regenerative micromanagement.

## Plan review and improvement

The initial V2 plan placed the Bot, Group, and Channel URLs in the image. Review rejected this because static-image URLs are not clickable and create three extra reading starts. The improved plan keeps the image to one offer sequence and puts all exact links in the caption.
