# AuriX Public Launch V3 — Locked Plan

## Audience and job

Audience: Myanmar users who already need VPN access for work, study, selling, creating, development, or everyday online access.

Job: announce that AuriX is publicly available and make the product/destination understood immediately.

## Message hierarchy

1. `AuriX VPN ကို အခုပဲ စတင်အသုံးပြုနိုင်ပါပြီ`
2. `Outline VPN Key ကို AuriX Bot ကနေ တိုက်ရိုက်ရယူနိုင်ပါတယ်`
3. `ပုံမှန်ဆို ပြေစာစစ်ချိန် ၁ မိနစ်အောက်*`
4. `@aurix_outline_vpn_bot`

Plans, wallets, free allowance, support links, and caveats remain in the caption.

## Creative exploration

- Documentary before/after — rejected for launch: reads as a problem explainer and feels small in ambition.
- Bot flow diagram — rejected: infographic/dashboard character.
- Generic server spectacle — rejected: category cliché and unsupported performance implication.
- **Monumental A-gateway unveiling — selected:** a generated architectural gateway and aurora path create scale, arrival, and public-launch energy while remaining specific to AuriX's brand motif.

## Plan council improvement

The first language plan began with the customer's frustration. Review found that it buried the launch. The revised plan starts with availability, explains the product in one sentence, gives one operational reason to act, and ends at the Bot. The image communicates confidence through scale rather than unverifiable adjectives.
