# AuriX Three-Plan Ad — Natural Burmese Copy Review

Campaign stamp: `20260829-201328`

## User-language direction

The revision keeps the customer's preferred vocabulary and removes the earlier, more abstract question-led framing.

- `Outline VPN 1 month key များ` → `Outline VPN တစ်လသုံး Key များ`
- `စိတ်ကြိုက် Plan ရွေးယူနိုင်ပါပြီ` retained as the main offer hook.
- `အစမ်း Free Plan` → `အစမ်းသုံး Free Plan` for a complete, natural label.
- `ပုံမှန် တစ်လသုံး` retained as the familiar middle choice.
- `အဝ တစ်လသုံး` → `အဝသုံး တစ်လ Plan` to read as one natural phrase.
- `စုံစမ်း မေးမြန်း ဝယ်ယူရန်` → `စုံစမ်းမေးမြန်းရန် · ဝယ်ယူရန်` for a smoother call to action.

## Review 1 — naturalness

The original user phrasing communicates the offer clearly but mixes English word order with Burmese. Reordering `1 month key` to `တစ်လသုံး Key` makes the product duration immediately understandable. Adding `သုံး` to the trial label makes it sound spoken rather than abbreviated.

## Review 2 — claim clarity

`အဝသုံး` can be mistaken for unlimited use when separated from the quota. The final image therefore keeps `100 GB` as the dominant card value. The caption explicitly states that the plan is capped at 100 GB and that all displayed GB values are VPN-key usage allowances, not phone SIM data.

## Final locked image copy

- `OUTLINE VPN · တစ်လသုံး KEY များ`
- `စိတ်ကြိုက် Plan ရွေးယူနိုင်ပါပြီ။`
- `အစမ်းသုံး Free Plan · 3 GB · အခမဲ့`
- `ပုံမှန် တစ်လသုံး · 50 GB · 3,000 ကျပ်`
- `အဝသုံး တစ်လ Plan · 100 GB · 6,000 ကျပ်`
- `စုံစမ်းမေးမြန်းရန် · ဝယ်ယူရန်`
- `AuriX Telegram Chat Group`

## Decision

Approved as the natural-language alternate. It preserves the established physical-pass campaign visual, avoids fake buttons and webpage-card styling, and uses the customer's preferred terms without introducing an unlimited-data claim.
