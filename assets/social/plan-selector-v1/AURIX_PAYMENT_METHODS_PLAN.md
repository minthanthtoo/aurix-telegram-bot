# AuriX Three-Plan Ad — Payment Methods Plan

## Evidence and commercial brief

- Audience: Myanmar customers who understand the plans but need confidence that their usual wallet is accepted.
- Job: reduce payment-method uncertainty at the conversion point.
- Promise: customers can transfer through KBZPay, WavePay, AYA Pay, uabpay, or CB Pay.
- Product memory: three AuriX Outline-compatible Key plans plus five familiar transfer options.
- CTA: ask or buy through the AuriX Telegram Chat Group.
- User-provided fact: AuriX accepts transfers through the five listed wallets.
- Operational truth: receipt review remains staff-assisted; the wallets are not presented as automated integrations or partners.

## Explored territories

1. White payment card copied from the reference — rejected because it would resemble a webpage pricing block and weaken the AuriX dark campaign system.
2. Five floating wallet stickers — rejected because it adds decorative clutter and generic collage energy.
3. Physical wallet-acceptance rail — selected because it extends the existing plan-pass material, keeps all five marks equal, and remains scannable on mobile.

## Plan council and improvement

The first plan placed the five logos beside the Telegram destination. The council judged that payment recognition and purchase destination would compete. The revised plan gives the wallets one compact rail below the plans and keeps Telegram on a separate final line. App names are typeset beneath official icons so recognition does not depend on icon familiarity alone.

## Locked hierarchy

1. AuriX and Outline Client relationship.
2. Plan choice and exact quota/price.
3. Accepted transfer methods.
4. Telegram destination.

No fake payment buttons, wallet balances, QR codes, partnership claims, savings claims, or instant-activation claims are permitted.
