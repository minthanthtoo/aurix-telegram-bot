## Plan council — review 1

### Strategy

The three offers belong together because the post's single job is choice clarification. The audience is price-aware and may not know whether to try first or buy immediately.

### Local language

Use `ရက် 30 တိုင်း` for the free 3 GB cadence, not `လစဉ်`; use `ကျပ်`, not `MMK`, in the main Burmese design. Use `အရင်စမ်းမယ်`, `ပုံမှန်သုံးမယ်`, and `ပိုသုံးမယ်` as concise customer-led cues.

### Mobile UX

A literal landscape remake would shrink Burmese text. Use three tall physical plan passes in portrait, each with an oversized quota and price. No controls or fake buttons.

### Brand and co-brand hierarchy

AuriX logo remains top-left and dominant. Outline Client icon sits top-right at smaller scale with `OUTLINE CLIENT` and the relationship line `AuriX key · Outline Client ဖြင့် အသုံးပြုရန်`. Telegram appears only in the footer destination area.

### Trust

Do not add savings, popularity, best-value, speed, security, or unlimited claims. Caption must clarify independent service and lack of official partnership.
