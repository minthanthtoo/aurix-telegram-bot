# AuriX Three-Plan Ad — Payment Methods Final Review

Campaign stamp: `20260830-013406`

## Source decision

The five marks come from current developer-owned Google Play listings cross-checked against each provider's official website or product page. Current app icons were selected instead of third-party vector recreations because they are first-party, consistent at equal square sizes, and highly recognizable to the target mobile audience. Deterministic labels preserve recognition where an icon alone may be unfamiliar.

## R0 review

| Criterion | Score | Judgment |
|---|---:|---|
| First-frame clarity | 4 | Plans and payment methods are visible, but the smaller icons and vertical dividers feel dense. |
| Product memory | 5 | AuriX, Outline Client, plans, and wallet options are retained. |
| Brand specificity | 4 | The rail matches the palette, though the equal upright cards still resemble a comparison layout. |
| Local relevance | 5 | Familiar wallets and Burmese purchase language fit the audience. |
| Visual distinction | 4 | Not a webpage, but the payment band is still somewhat conventional. |
| Copy hierarchy | 4 | The long list above the same list of icons is repetitive. |
| Claim safety | 5 | No partnership or automatic-payment claim. |
| Render integrity | 5 | All official icons render correctly. |

Decision: enlarge the icons, remove column dividers, and make the plan passes more physical.

## R1 review

| Criterion | Score | Judgment |
|---|---:|---|
| First-frame clarity | 5 | Larger marks scan quickly. |
| Product memory | 5 | Plan and payment recognition remain balanced. |
| Brand specificity | 5 | Staggered physical passes and matching acceptance rail belong to the campaign. |
| Local relevance | 5 | Wallet names match current local app naming. |
| Visual distinction | 5 | No fake interface or payment buttons. |

| Claim safety | 5 | The caption explains staff review and no partner implication. |
| Render integrity | 4 | Large ticket punches attract too much attention between cards. |

Decision: reduce ticket punches and replace the repeated provider list with one natural Burmese line.

## R2 final

The final uses `Wallet ၅ မျိုးနဲ့ ငွေလွှဲနိုင်ပါတယ်` as a short scan label, then lets the five official icons and names carry recognition. Payment methods are clearly secondary to the offer, while Telegram remains the final purchase destination. The caption states that receipt approval is staff-assisted and that displaying wallet marks does not imply formal partnership.

## Publication gate

Before publication, confirm that all five recipient accounts are currently operational, staff can distinguish and verify receipts from each wallet, and the advertised paid plans are available.
