# AuriX Three-Plan Reference Ad — Final Review

Campaign stamp: `20260829-184302`  
Reference retained at: `reference/supplied-outline-three-plan-reference.png`

## Reference adaptation

Kept from the supplied reference:

- immediate three-choice comparison;
- oversized quota and price anchors;
- numbered progression;
- one visually emphasized paid choice;
- Outline Client recognition.

Deliberately changed:

- portrait mobile-first format instead of landscape;
- physical plan-pass construction instead of webpage pricing cards;
- AuriX color and typography system instead of generic black/green VPN styling;
- no fake `SELECT PLAN` buttons;
- no savings, best-value, popularity, speed, unlimited, or security claims;
- correct AuriX offer facts and rolling-30-day language;
- AuriX primary brand, Outline Client secondary compatibility mark, Telegram community destination.

## Plan review

The initial idea used three equal cards. The council judged that it would copy the reference too literally and repeat a web-pricing pattern. The revised plan locked three clipped-corner physical passes, customer-led cues, a center-lifted 50 GB paid choice, and a non-button Telegram destination.

## Asset review 1 — R0

| Criterion | Score | Judgment |
|---|---:|---|
| First-frame clarity | 5 | All three quotas and prices scan quickly. |
| Product memory | 5 | AuriX, Outline Client, and the three offers are visible. |
| Brand specificity | 4 | Palette is AuriX-specific, but aligned panels still resembled pricing UI. |
| Local relevance | 5 | Burmese cues and local-price formatting are natural. |
| Visual distinction | 3 | Equal aligned panels retained too much webpage-card grammar. |
| Copy hierarchy | 4 | Relationship line floated between header and plans. |
| Claim safety | 5 | No unsupported claims. |
| Render integrity | 5 | Logos, type, links, and dimensions are correct. |

Decision: improve physicality and hierarchy. Add ticket notches, slight rotation/stagger, center lift, and move the relationship line under the hook.

## Asset review 2 — R1

| Criterion | Score | Judgment |
|---|---:|---|
| First-frame clarity | 5 | Offer scan remains strong. |
| Product memory | 5 | 3 GB free and both paid choices are clear. |
| Brand specificity | 5 | Physical passes and AuriX accents feel campaign-specific. |
| Local relevance | 5 | Audience-led cues are conversational and concise. |
| Visual distinction | 5 | No longer resembles a browser or app screen. |
| Copy hierarchy | 5 | Brand → hook → relationship → plans → Telegram is clear. |
| Claim safety | 5 | Center emphasis does not claim popularity or superior value. |
| Render integrity | 4 | Large punch notches created distracting dark dots between plans. |

Decision: preserve the direction and reduce only the notch size/perforation contrast. Slightly strengthen the AuriX logo anchor.

## Final — R2

The final retains the physical-pass concept while keeping the offer dominant. The 50 GB plan is the primary paid decision anchor; the 3 GB free plan remains a low-risk entry and the 100 GB plan remains an honest heavier-use option.
