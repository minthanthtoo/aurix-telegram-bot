# Receipt Confidence V1 — Creative Plan

## Job

Convert paid-intent buyers who do not want to wait for a seller to manually review an uploaded payment receipt.

## Audience and message

- Audience: Myanmar users already willing to pay for a dependable Outline VPN Key.
- Friction: waiting for the seller to review the uploaded receipt before the Outline VPN Key can be issued.
- User-provided product promise: submit a readable receipt image; AI checks it automatically and a successful check clears the review state so the Key can be issued almost immediately.
- Hook: `Outline VPN Key ဝယ်ပြီး / ပြေစာစစ်ပေးမယ့်အချိန် / စောင့်နေရသေးလား?`
- Proof: `ပြေစာပုံ ပို့လိုက်ရုံ / AI က အလိုအလျောက် စစ်ပေး / အတည်ပြုပြီးတာနဲ့ Key ရပြီ`

## Visual hierarchy

1. One question-led hook at the top.
2. One real-world receipt-to-envelope process scene.
3. One connected proof label pointing to the approved AuriX Telegram Bot avatar.
4. Five accepted wallet marks in one restrained footer strip.

The AuriX mark is primary. Outline is a smaller product cue. Telegram is an attached modifier on the Bot avatar, not a competing generic logo tile.

## Guardrails

- No fake chat, fake scanner interface, fake CTA button, stopwatch, or automatic-payment-approval claim.
- Do not use a hard seconds or sub-one-minute guarantee; express the benefit as avoiding seller-wait and receiving the Key shortly after a successful automated check.
- The checked-in code still routes receipt evidence to human verification. Publishing R3 is gated on the automated review-and-fulfillment path being live in the deployed Bot.
- The image carries one message; plan prices and operational caveats stay in the caption.
- Myanmar copy must use generous mark clearance and be checked at 324 × 405 px.

## Production method

The text-free editorial plate was generated once, then the approved logos, exact Burmese copy, wallet marks, and typographic hierarchy were composed deterministically in SVG. R0–R2 changed only hierarchy, connection, and spacing—not the core concept.
