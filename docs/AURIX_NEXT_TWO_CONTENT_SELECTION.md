# AuriX Next-Two Content Selection

Date: 2026-09-01

Reference standard: `AURIX-SOCIAL-S1`

## Ranking method

Every direction was scored for the next production slot using:

- qualified paid or repeat revenue: 30%;
- activation, retention, or support-load reduction: 20%;
- AuriX-specific differentiation: 20%;
- factual and operational readiness: 15%;
- three-second mobile creative clarity: 10%;
- production leverage from approved assets: 5%.

The numbers express relative production priority, not forecast revenue or audience size.

## Selected 1 — Receipt confidence

Role: highest-priority general ad.

### Audience and job

Paid-intent Myanmar customers who understand the plan but do not want to wait for a seller to manually review the uploaded transaction receipt.

### Locked image copy

1. `Outline VPN Key ဝယ်ပြီး`
2. `ပြေစာစစ်ပေးမယ့်အချိန် စောင့်နေရသေးလား?`
3. `ပြေစာပုံ ပို့လိုက်ရုံ`
4. `AI က အလိုအလျောက် စစ်ပေး`
5. `အတည်ပြုပြီးတာနဲ့ Key ရပြီ`
6. `@aurix_outline_vpn_bot`

Payment heading, if used: `Wallet ၅ မျိုးနဲ့ လွှဲနိုင်`

### Reading path

`seller-review wait → receipt upload → automatic AI check → successful confirmation → Key fulfillment → AuriX Bot destination → accepted wallets`

### Selected visual mechanism

An overhead real-world payment desk. A physical receipt enters one cyan reading beam; the readable fields become a restrained abstract signal, not a fake OCR screen. After a distinct human approval mark, the path reaches a sealed AuriX Key envelope. The five official wallet marks form one quiet footer rail.

The AuriX V4 Bot avatar with its Telegram badge anchors the action path. The official Outline Client icon remains a smaller product-recognition cue.

### Claim boundary

- User-provided target behavior: AI automatically checks uploaded receipts; a successful check clears review and allows near-immediate Key fulfillment.
- Do not promise an exact number of seconds or guaranteed delivery under one minute.
- The current checked-in implementation still requires human verification. Publishing the corrected R3 creative is gated on live verification of the automated review-and-fulfillment path.
- If the automatic check cannot confirm an order, the caption may say the Admin Team will help; do not make fraud detection the customer-facing message.

### Reject

- fake Telegram conversation;
- OCR dashboard or scanner interface;
- green confirmation button;
- stopwatch or speed badge;
- robotic hand approving payment;
- wallet logos equal to or larger than AuriX.

### Success metric

Orders that progress from creation to a valid receipt submission, followed by verified paid orders.

## Selected 2 — Telegram Bot quota-control ad

Role: dedicated AuriX Telegram Bot advertisement.

This direction is selected over a generic Bot launch because it gives the Bot a unique product memory: it is where a customer checks an AuriX Key and receives quota reminders. Telegram is part of the behavior, not merely the destination logo.

### Audience and job

Existing Outline users, freelancers, online operators, and technically confident customers who dislike guessing how much AuriX VPN allowance remains.

### Locked image copy

Hook:

1. `Outline VPN Quota`
2. `ဘယ်လောက်ကျန်လဲ?`

Proof:

3. `AuriX Bot မှာ အချိန်မရွေး စစ်နိုင်`
4. `25% · 10% · 5% ကျန်တိုင်း Telegram က အသိပေး`

Destination:

5. `@aurix_outline_vpn_bot`

### Reading path

`quota uncertainty → anytime lookup → three advance reminder points → large AuriX Telegram Bot identity → destination → optional wallet reassurance`

### Selected visual mechanism

Upgrade the authored precision-instrument territory from `quota-control-payments-v1`. Use one calibrated A-shaped signal path with three physical notches at 25%, 10%, and 5%. The AuriX V4 Bot avatar plus attached Telegram badge is a large focal object connected directly to the measured path. The official Outline icon sits beside the quota question as a secondary recognition anchor.

This is an authored editorial instrument, not a dashboard, speedometer, phone screenshot, or generated cyber background. If payment marks are included, they stay in a normalized footer strip and do not interrupt the quota story.

### Claim boundary

- The displayed thresholds must remain exactly 25%, 10%, and 5% unless the implementation and tests change.
- Usage is the latest available AuriX/Outline transfer observation, not live speed telemetry.
- Do not imply uninterrupted access, universal reliability, low latency, or automatic renewal.

### Reject

- fake quota dashboard;
- generic glowing server room;
- unrelated AI background;
- tiny or naive Bot badge;
- small scattered feature labels;
- five wallets competing with the quota proof;
- the unsupported 50%/10%/0% sequence.

### Success metric

Qualified Bot starts, My VPN opens, and paid users returning to inspect their Key state.

## Why the other high-ranked directions wait

- Expiry/renewal is the next retention asset, but it works best after viewers have learned that My VPN exists.
- Setup rescue protects activation, but it should follow the receipt post so the sequence mirrors the customer journey.
- Plan choice already has usable assets; refreshing it now would add less new product memory than the selected pair.

## Recommended publishing sequence

1. Receipt confidence — paid conversion.
2. Setup or educational post from the existing library — visual and funnel breathing room.
3. Telegram Bot quota control — product differentiation.
4. Expiry/renewal — retention follow-up.

Do not publish the two selected ads back-to-back as if they were duplicate Bot announcements.

## Council record

The external council agreed with the two selections. Its ranking feedback was used only as critique.

Accepted:

- receipt confidence is closest to the paid conversion step;
- quota lookup and threshold reminders are stronger Bot differentiators than a generic launch claim;
- receipt confidence should connect naturally to later setup support;
- the quota ad should stay practical rather than becoming a technical specification sheet.

Rejected as unsupported:

- claims about local fraud prevalence;
- assumptions that the technical audience has higher average revenue;
- assumptions about free-tier infrastructure cost or cannibalization without analytics;
- claims about connectivity volatility as campaign evidence.
