## What this standard means

S1 is the approved grammar for audience-situation posts that move a Facebook viewer toward the AuriX Telegram Bot. It is not a universal template and must not make every AuriX post look identical. Pricing comparisons, setup guides, giveaways, service notices, and testimonials may need different composition systems, but they must still inherit the brand, language, and validation rules below.

## Locked job

- Start with one recognizable audience situation.
- Connect the situation to one AuriX product behavior or proof.
- Make the Telegram destination unmistakable without adding fake controls.
- Keep AuriX primary, Outline secondary, and payment marks tertiary.
- Let the caption carry operational detail, terms, caveats, and secondary links.

## Canonical reading path

`audience moment → practical tension → one product proof → AuriX Telegram Bot identity → destination → optional payment reassurance`

The R4 reference implements this as:

`Customer စာပြန်ချိန် → VPN လိုက်စမ်းရတာ မောနေပြီလား? → Quota ဘယ်လောက်ကျန်လဲ? → အချိန်မရွေး ဝင်စစ်နိုင် → AuriX Bot + Telegram badge → @aurix_outline_vpn_bot → wallet strip`

## Composition rules

1. Use a 1080 × 1350 portrait canvas and preserve 72–96 px outer safe margins.
2. Give the hook one dominant region. Do not distribute small labels evenly across the canvas.
3. Use one concrete scene or authored visual mechanism that belongs to the audience situation.
4. Put the product proof on an object or region that belongs to that scene. The R4 parcel label is an example, not a required shape.
5. Keep one uninterrupted area of negative space around the hook.
6. Use the approved V2 AuriX identity as the primary mark and the official Outline Client icon as a smaller recognition anchor.
7. When Telegram recognition matters, pair the approved V4 Bot avatar with one attached Telegram-blue badge at roughly 32–42% of the avatar diameter. Keep the badge subordinate to AuriX and pair the cluster with the real bot handle.
8. Add payment marks only when they remove purchase friction. Normalize official artwork inside equal recognition cells; never use them as decoration.
9. A handle or URL is a destination cue. Do not draw a non-working `Buy`, `Select`, or `Start` button.

## Copy rules

- One hook, one proof, and one destination are the default maximum.
- Draft directly in conversational Burmese; do not translate English ad grammar word for word.
- Prefer a familiar question or consequence over a feature heading.
- Preserve product qualifiers. If a benefit applies only to Keys bought through AuriX, say so.
- Do not call VPN allowance SIM data or mobile data.
- Do not claim speed, low latency, uptime, security, automatic payment approval, popularity, or partnership without current evidence.
- Keep exact prices, quotas, durations, thresholds, and eligibility in deterministic text.

## Myanmar typography rules

- Use a tested Noto Sans Myanmar family and real installed weights.
- Add `xml:lang="my"`; keep `mark` and `mkmk` shaping enabled.
- Use Myanmar-aware baseline clearance. Never tighten leading to solve overflow.
- Avoid outline strokes around combining marks. Use weight, scale, color, a restrained shadow, or one semantic underline instead.
- Inspect the production image and a 324 × 405 feed preview.
- Run the strict Myanmar SVG audit before selection.

## Production split

- Image generation may own a coherent text-free scene, object, material, lighting, and negative-space plan.
- SVG/layout code owns all Burmese copy, prices, quotas, handles, official marks, disclaimers, and payment assets.
- Regenerate when the scene mechanism fails. Recompose when only copy hierarchy, spacing, or logo treatment fails.
- Run one plan review, then no more than two focused asset improvement loops unless a factual or rendering defect remains.

## Required final bundle

- 1080 × 1350 PNG;
- UTF-8 caption TXT;
- editable SVG or equivalent deterministic source;
- 324 × 405 preview;
- manifest with hashes, canonical asset paths, and publication gates;
- brief final review recording reading path and rejected claims.

## Rejection gates

Reject the asset if any of these are true:

- it resembles a webpage, dashboard, or fake Telegram interface;
- the viewer remembers Outline or a wallet more strongly than AuriX;
- the Telegram Bot identity is ambiguous;
- several small labels create competing reading starts;
- the scene is generic AI technology art unrelated to the audience occasion;
- the Burmese copy is technically legible but unnatural;
- a key promise depends on a live operation that has not been verified.

## Future-agent instruction

Start from this standard's decisions, not by copying the R4 coordinates. Lock the audience job and reading path first, select a new visual mechanism appropriate to that job, and preserve the hierarchy and proof discipline. A new post must feel related to R4 without becoming a recolored duplicate of it.
